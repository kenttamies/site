Offline Games: Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026. Applies to the game collection Offline Games ("Offline Games: 21 Classics" on the App Store) on iPhone, its Android version, and its web test version at offline.kenttami.es.

In short

  • Offline Games collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The games work without an internet connection and don't send anything you do in them to us or anyone else.
  • Your saved games, records, daily streaks and settings stay on your own device.
  • The one-off unlock is bought through Apple (or Google on Android). We never see your name or payment details.

Who is responsible

Offline Games is made by Miika Kenttämies, an independent developer in Sweden, who is the data controller for anything described here. Contact: wth@kenttami.es.

What stays on your device

Games, records and settings

Games in progress, your personal leaderboards, statistics, daily challenge results and settings are saved on your device only (in the browser's storage for the web version). They are not sent to us. If your device's own backup is turned on (such as iCloud Backup or Android backup), they are included in that backup, which is handled by Apple or Google. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes them.

Free trial

To run the 3-day free trial, the app stores the time it was first opened and the latest time it has seen. On iPhone these are kept in the device's Keychain so that reinstalling the app doesn't restart the trial; on Android they are kept in the app's storage. They never leave your device or its backups.

Purchases

The "Unlock all games" purchase is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store (or by Google through Google Play). Apple or Google handles your payment under its own privacy policy. The app only learns whether the unlock has been bought on your account; we do not receive your name, Apple ID, email address or payment details.

Web version

The web test version at offline.kenttami.es is hosted on GitHub Pages. To deliver the site, GitHub processes your IP address and may keep it in its server logs under its own privacy policy. The web version has no cookies, analytics or ads, and keeps your games in your browser's storage.

What the app doesn't do

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising, advertising identifiers or tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or other data sent to us.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Children

The games are suitable for all ages. Because no personal data is collected, none is collected from children either.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Everything the app stores is on your own device, and you can delete it by deleting the app or clearing the site's data. If you live in the EU or EEA you can still contact us with any privacy question, and you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, imy.se) or to the authority where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
