Common questions

How does the free trial work? Every game is open for 3 days from the first time you open the app. After that, one purchase ("Unlock all games") keeps every game open forever. There are no ads and no subscription.

I bought the unlock on another device. Open the app, tap Unlock, then tap "Restore purchase". Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Will I lose my games if I delete the app? Saved games and records are kept on your device only, so deleting the app deletes them. They are included in your device's own backup (such as iCloud Backup) if it is turned on.

How do I play a game? Every game has a "How to play" button (the question mark at the top right).
