Brrrally: Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026. Applies to the game Brrrally (formerly VC Racing) on iPhone and iPad, its web version at vcracing.kenttami.es, and its test builds (including TestFlight).

In short

  • The game has no accounts and no ads. It does not sell your data, share it for advertising, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • Your progress and settings are kept on your own device.
  • If you pick a player name, it is shown to other players in online races and on the leaderboards, together with your times.
  • Anonymous play statistics are sent only if you say yes, and you can switch them off at any time in Settings.

Who is responsible

Brrrally is made by Miika Kenttämies, an independent developer in Sweden, who is the data controller for the processing described here. Contact details for privacy requests will be published on this page.

What the game processes, and why

1. Your progress and settings (on your device only)

Medals, cash, gold, cars, paint jobs, settings and your player name are saved on your device (in the browser's storage for the web version). They are not sent to us. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes them.

2. Player name and leaderboards

When you choose a player name and post a time, the game sends: a random player ID created by the game, your player name, the stage, the car and your time. For the Daily Stage it also sends a recording of your car's path, so that other players can race it as a "ghost". These are shown publicly on the leaderboards in the game.

  • Why and legal basis: to run the leaderboards and ghosts you take part in (performance of the service you use, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and to keep them fair (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Retention: Daily Stage runs are deleted automatically after 8 days. Each player's best time per board is kept for as long as the leaderboard exists, or until you ask for it to be deleted.
  • Where: stored with Cloudflare (Workers and D1). Your IP address is used for a moment to limit how many times can be posted per minute; it is not stored by us.
  • Choose a name that does not identify you if you prefer. Do not use your real name if you are a child.

3. Online races

Online races run through Photon (Exit Games GmbH, Germany), on its servers in the EU. During a race your player name, your car and its paint, and your car's position are sent to the other players in the room. Photon processes your IP address to connect you. We do not store race traffic.

  • Legal basis: performance of the service you use (Art. 6(1)(b)).

4. Play statistics (only with your consent)

The first time you play, the game asks whether it may send play data to Unity Analytics (Unity Technologies, USA). Only if you say yes, it sends: play sessions, the modes, stages and cars you race, finishing places and races left early, together with a random installation ID and device details (such as model, operating system, language and an approximate country). No name or account is attached. We use it to see which parts of the game are played and where players get stuck.

  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time in Settings; the game then stops sending play data.
  • Retention: as set by Unity Analytics; see Unity's privacy policy at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

5. The web version and this website

The web version is served from Amazon Web Services in Stockholm, Sweden. Like any web server it keeps short-lived technical logs (IP address, time, the file requested, browser type) to deliver the site and keep it secure (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)). The game itself uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

6. App stores and TestFlight

Downloads, updates and any purchases go through Apple's App Store or Google Play, under their own privacy policies. If you test a build through TestFlight, Apple shares with us the name and email address you were invited with, your device model and system version, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. We use these only to run the test and fix problems, and delete them when they are no longer needed for that.

What the game does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or "sharing" of personal information, as those words are used in US state privacy laws.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.
  • No accounts, passwords or payment details handled by the game.

Who else processes data

Only the service providers named above (Cloudflare, Exit Games/Photon, Unity, Amazon Web Services) and the app stores, each for the purpose described. Some of them are outside the EU or operate worldwide (Unity and Cloudflare in the USA). Such transfers are protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (as the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act defines it). In Sweden and the EU, consent to play statistics can be given from age 13. If you believe a child has posted personal information, for example their real name as a player name, ask us to delete it and we will.

Your rights

In the EU and EEA (GDPR) you have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its processing, receive it in a portable form, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. You can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, imy.se) or to the authority where you live.

In the United States, residents of states with privacy laws (such as California under the CCPA/CPRA) have the right to know what personal information is collected, to access, correct and delete it, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information and do not use sensitive personal information. Because there is no such selling or sharing, a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal changes nothing in how the game behaves.

To use these rights, for example to have your leaderboard entries deleted, contact us (see "Who is responsible") and include your player name. Data on your own device you can delete yourself by deleting the app or clearing the site's data.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and kept with the providers above, who protect it under their own security programs. No system is perfectly secure, which is one reason the game collects so little.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the game or its store listing.
